時を纏う芸術
The Story

More than a garment. A living archive.

For centuries, the kimono has carried stories through silk, color, pattern, and form. Every detail reflects a dialogue between nature, craftsmanship, identity, and time.

This exhibition invites you to look beyond the garment and discover the stories woven into it.
